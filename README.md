# 📊 Hospital Emergency Room Performance Dashboard

An interactive, data-driven Excel dashboard designed to analyze and monitor monthly **Emergency Room (ER) operational metrics, patient flow, and satisfaction benchmarks**. This tool translates raw medical administration data into actionable insights for hospital leadership to optimize staffing, reduce wait times, and improve patient care.

---

## 🔎 Key Insights & Performance Metrics
*   **Total Volume:** 513 Patients admitted or processed through triage.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** An average wait time of 36.32 minutes.
*   **Patient Experience:** A high satisfaction score of 4.96 out of 5.0.

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations & Breakdown
*   **Throughput Analysis:** 62% of patients were treated Ontime, while 38% experienced service delivery Delays. 52% of patients were formally admitted to the hospital.
*   **Patient Demographics:** Balanced distribution consisting of 53% Female and 47% Male patients, with distinct volume peaks in the 0–9 and 60–69 age brackets.
*   **Departmental Referral Trends:** Categorizes patient volume by final specialty clinic routing, showing major volumes in General Practice (103) and Orthopedics (65).

## 🛠️ Technical Features Used
*   **Data Modeling:** Cleaned and structured raw transactional hospital logs into operational tables.
*   **Dynamic Slicers:** Interactive timelines for time-series filtering across years and individual months.
*   **Data Visualization:** Implemented a unified color hierarchy using targeted donut charts, bar graphs, and horizontal distribution charts.
